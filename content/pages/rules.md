---
title: Rules of CodeVerse Hub
---
**R1❯ Follow Discord Terms & Guidelines**

All server rules are based on Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
Modded clients like Vencord or BetterDiscord are allowed.

**R2❯ No Raids, Doxxing, or Swatting**

Do not participate in, joke about, or threaten raids, doxxing, or swatting.
Any such threat will result in a permanent ban.
Sharing information related to these actions is also prohibited, even “for awareness”.
You may discuss incidents for awareness only, without encouraging the behavior.

**R3❯ No Scams, Malware, or Malicious Links**

Using Discord for scams, malware, viruses, IP grabbers, or harmful links is strictly prohibited.
This applies both inside the server and in DMs.
Cybersecurity discussions are allowed only in appropriate channels.
Do not share malware, source code, tutorials, tools, or stream such content.

**R4❯ No Sharing Personal Information**

Leaking or sharing personal information is not allowed.
This includes IP addresses, phone numbers, addresses, legal names, IDs, or similar data.

**R5❯ Voice Channel Conduct**

Prohibited behavior in voice channels includes:
Racism, sexism, hate speech, ToS violations, earrape, or disruptive behavior.
Recording or archiving conversations without owner permission is not allowed.

**R6❯ No NSFW, Gore, or Shock Content**

NSFW content, gore, shock content, or explicit behavior is strictly prohibited.
This includes jokes or indirect references to such content.
Racism and sexism are also not allowed.

**R7❯ No Politics or Religious Debates**

Politics and religion are not topics for this server.
Any offensive content toward countries, institutions, or beliefs is prohibited.
Any reference to Nazis/Hitler or equivalents will result in a permanent ban.

**R8❯ Profiles Must Follow Rules**

Your Discord profile must comply with server rules and Discord ToS.
Names, bios, avatars, pronouns, and statuses can be moderated.
NSFW content, hate speech, impersonation, or malicious content will be punished.

**R9❯ No Advertising**

Advertising is not allowed on this server.
DM advertising without a valid reason is prohibited and can result in a ban.
If someone asks for a link, share it via DM only.
Projects may be shared only in the appropriate channels.

**R10❯ No Rule Loopholes / Use Common Sense**

These rules are not exhaustive.
Trying to bypass rules is not allowed.
If unsure, ask staff via tickets or do not proceed.

**R11❯ Respect Moderation Decisions**

Do not disrupt the server if you disagree with a punishment.
Appeal respectfully through proper channels.
If bias is suspected after appeal, contact staff in this order via tickets/modmail only:
Head Mod → Admin → Owner
Do not DM staff or add them without consent.

**TL;DR**
Use common sense, respect others, and act maturely.
